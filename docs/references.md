## Use and limits

| Source | Recorded inspection scope | Supported use in this project |
|---|---|---|
| Watkins and Dayan (1992) | Saved abstract and introductory passages | Action-value learning and conditional convergence context; no finite-run convergence claim |
| Alshiekh et al. (2018) | Saved abstract and introductory passages | Action intervention and formal shielding context; the implemented immediate checks do not inherit synthesized temporal-logic guarantees |
| Patterson et al. (2024) | Saved abstract and introductory passages | Empirical choices, variation, hyperparameters, and experimental bias; project-specific conclusions require its own evidence |
| Russell and Norvig (2021), Chapter 22 | All 34 supplied chapter-page screenshots reviewed October 11; publication metadata checked against Pearson front matter | Model-free Q-learning, external constraints, generalization, approximation risks, and future-work context; project-specific failure mechanisms come from retained evidence |
| Module 7 instructional text | Course-provided instructional content | Multi-agent concepts and scalability context; three nodes managed by one learner do not establish MARL |

These inspection records do not establish complete review of every original paper. The full Part 1 bibliography remains in the unchanged submitted report. Private course attachments are excluded from this repository. This documentation revision checks consistency with the retained source records; it does not claim a new independent bibliographic or full-text audit.
