# Background sources used in presentation

This reference record contains public publication links and inspected claim scope, not copies of private course attachments.

- Watkins, C. J. C. H., & Dayan, P. (1992). Q-learning. *Machine Learning, 8*, 279–292. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00992698. Original saved paper abstract/introduction inspected for experienced action values and conditional convergence. No finite-run convergence claim made.
- Alshiekh, M., Bloem, R., Ehlers, R., Könighofer, B., Niekum, S., & Topcu, U. (2018). Safe reinforcement learning via shielding. *Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 32*(1), 2669–2678. https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v32i1.11797. Original saved paper abstract/introduction inspected for synthesized temporal-logic shields and action intervention. Project implements immediate modeled checks, not that formal guarantee.
- Patterson, A., Neumann, S., White, M., & White, A. (2024). Empirical design in reinforcement learning. *Journal of Machine Learning Research, 25*(318), 1–63. https://jmlr.org/papers/v25/23-0183.html. Original saved paper abstract/introduction inspected for empirical choices, variation, hyperparameters, and experimenter bias.
- Mohammad, A. F. (2026). *Multi-Agent RL Applications* [PhDAI-830 Module 7 instructional text]. University of the Cumberlands. Course-provided context; three nodes with one learning controller do not establish a MARL implementation. Private course text is excluded from this package.

No source supplies this project's performance numbers; those come from retained logs and policies. Full original-paper review and Keith's independent understanding are separate from these bounded source inspections.
